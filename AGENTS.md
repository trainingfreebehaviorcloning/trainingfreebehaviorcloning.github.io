# Website publication state

The public GitHub Pages site publishes from the root of main. Use localhost to review drafts before pushing to main.

- Preserve the original research figures and PowerPoint assets. Use the palette
  recorded in `palette-provenance.json` for site accents and heading gradients.
- Do not put the complete research presentation video back on the website.
  Use short, relevant clips from the supplied materials.
- Use short, muted inline video loops for demonstrations. Keep still posters for
  loading and reduced motion.
- Keep the method diagram large and centered, with descriptions underneath.
  Do not shrink it into a layout with descriptions beside it.
- Highlight connected arrows without adding outlines or white halos that cross
  the diagram's arrows and labels.

# Website copy

Use plain research prose and descriptive headings. Avoid slogans, promotional
claims, and captions about how website assets were assembled. Explain what the
method does and what each experiment shows; keep asset provenance in the
repository documentation.

Do not use centered dots as text separators. Use words or ordinary punctuation.
